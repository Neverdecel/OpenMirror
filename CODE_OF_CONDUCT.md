# Code of conduct

## Our standard

OpenMirror is built in public. Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Keep discussions in the open. Private side channels are how problems hide.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation in any form.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate derailing.
- Publishing others' private information without consent. This includes pasting real tokens or credentials into issues, which is also a security problem.
- Any conduct that makes participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Reporting

Reports go to the maintainers, privately, by email (address on the repo profile) or through GitHub's report flow. Every report is reviewed and answered.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will respond proportionately: a warning, removal of content, or exclusion from the project. Serious or repeated violations result in permanent removal. Enforcement decisions are final and are made public when they affect the record (content removed, people banned).

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public venue where someone is acting on the project's behalf.
